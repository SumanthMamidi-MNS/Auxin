# Orders and Payments Frequently Asked Questions

## Purpose
Answers common customer and farmer questions regarding payments, escrow, and cancellations.

## Content
Frequently asked questions regarding payment processing, escrow, and order modifications.
- **Q: Why is my payment status shown as 'Escrow Hold'?**
  A: Escrow holds funds safely until your order is delivered to your doorstep and verified via OTP.
- **Q: How long do I have to cancel an order?**
  A: Orders can be cancelled anytime before the farmer accepts or marks the order as 'Out for Delivery'.
- **Q: Where can I get an invoice for my purchase?**
  A: Digital invoices are accessible under 'Order History' -> 'View Receipt'.
