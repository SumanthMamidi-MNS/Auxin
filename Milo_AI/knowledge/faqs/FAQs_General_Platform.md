# General Platform Frequently Asked Questions

## Purpose
Answers common user questions regarding mobile accounts, password resets, and navigation.

## Content
Frequently asked questions regarding general platform usage.
- **Q: How do I reset my password?**
  A: Click 'Forgot Password' on the login screen. An OTP will be sent to your mobile number to set a new password.
- **Q: Can I use one account as both Customer and Farmer?**
  A: No. Separate mobile registration accounts are required to distinguish customer and farmer dashboards.
- **Q: Is the Auxin app available on iOS and Android?**
  A: Yes, Auxin is accessible via web browsers on mobile devices and responsive web apps.
