# 24/7 Customer Support Channels and MONSOON Emergency Protocol

## Purpose
Outlines support response SLAs, contact channels, and emergency weather delivery protocols.

## Content
Auxin provides round-the-clock support for all platform users.
- **24/7 Milo AI Support**: Milo AI provides instant support for orders, tracking, and platform navigation.
- **Support Escalation**: Human support agents resolve complex disputes within 2 hours.
- **Emergency MONSOON Protocol**: During extreme weather or monsoon alerts, delivery geofences dynamically adjust and delivery windows extend by 45 minutes for courier safety.

## Examples
- **Support SLA**: Immediate AI chat response; 2-hour human escalation SLA.

## Notes & Warnings
> [!IMPORTANT]
> During MONSOON protocol alerts, couriers receive additional safety allowances.

## Summary
Auxin offers 24/7 AI chat support, 2-hour human escalation SLAs, and MONSOON emergency safety protocols.
